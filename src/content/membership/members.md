Anyone may become a EuCAIF member by subscribing to the eucaif-info mailing list (see [Contact](/eucaif.org/contact/) page). Membership is intended as an open and lightweight way to stay connected with EuCAIF. Membership is open to individuals from the ECFA, NuPECC and APPEC communities and from related fields, including astronomy, AI research, and industry, where activities align with EuCAIF's mission.
