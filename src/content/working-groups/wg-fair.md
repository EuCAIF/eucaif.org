---
code: WG-FAIR
title: FAIR-ness & Sustainability
order: 3
---
Tentative mission/goals: (1) Review existing activities exist in fundamental physics that strive to make ML algorithms Findable, Available, Interoperable and Reproducible (may include ML datasets. (2) Understand what the barriers to making ML FAIR are - this includes availability of standardised tools and computing facilities for retraining or using someone else's model. (3) Discuss steps to facilitate and promote FAIR ML in fundamental science. (4) Start efforts to review and frame the environmental sustainability of ML algorithms (potential discussion of trade-off with performance)
