Specific requirements include:

- Internationally recognized, high-impact publications in their research field
- High-impact publications on AI methods relevant to fundamental physics, in either AI or physics venues
- A demonstrable track record of training young scientists advancing AI in fundamental physics
- Active and leading contributions to EuCAIF activities as a member, or a clear plan for such contributions presented in the application

Exceptional candidates who do not yet meet all criteria may still be considered.

To apply for a EuCAIF Fellowship, please complete the [Fellowship application form](#membership-application) at the bottom of this page. It asks for a short expression of interest (maximum half a page, about 250 words, excluding references) outlining how you envision contributing to EuCAIF and how you have contributed in the past to AI in fundamental physics. Applications are reviewed by three independent referees selected by the Management Board: one from the candidate's field, one from a related field, and one Management Board member. Final decisions are made by majority vote of all Management Board members.

Fellowships are granted for five years. Renewal is normally granted to fellows who demonstrate sustained contributions to EuCAIF, for example through regular meeting attendance and active participation in activities. Fellows who show limited or irregular engagement may be contacted by the Management Board to confirm their continued interest before renewal is decided.

Full details can be find in the EuCAIF policy document (see [Documents](/documents/) page).
