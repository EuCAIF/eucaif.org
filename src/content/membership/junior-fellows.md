---
title: EuCAIF junior fellows
order: 2
---
EuCAIF junior fellows are scientists at advanced early-career stages (typically tenure-track, or equivalent) who do not yet hold a tenured or equivalent permanent position at a European institution, who are on a trajectory toward such positions, and who demonstrate strong engagement with EuCAIF's mission. The same four criteria as for EuCAIF fellows (below) apply, but are assessed relative to career stage; in particular, evidence of potential may substitute for a full track record in areas such as training young scientists or providing scientific leadership. EuCAIF junior fellows automatically transition to EuCAIF fellows upon obtaining a tenured or equivalent permanent position at a European institution. Applications follow the same procedure as fellowship applications.
