---
title: How to stay in touch
---
If you like to follow the activities of EuCAIF please join the following e-group: [eucaif-info@cern.ch](mailto:eucaif-info@cern.ch)

How? If you would like to apply for membership of a CERN e-group, visit [http://cern.ch/egroups](http://cern.ch/egroups) and search for the e-group (e.g. eucaif-info) you would like to join.

To reach out to the management board, please write to [info@eucaif.org](mailto:info@eucaif.org)
