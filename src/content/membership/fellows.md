highlight: true

Fellows guide the strategic direction of EuCAIF, contribute actively to the implementation of its activities, and participate in regular meetings to take key decisions, including the election of Management Board members. Scientists holding permanent scientific positions at European institutions, or equivalent long-term appointments, with a strong track record and high visibility in AI and fundamental physics may apply for a (renewable) five-year fellowship. EuCAIF follows the broad definition of "Europe" used by JENAA and its supporting organisations (ECFA, NuPECC, and APPEC), which serves as the reference for eligibility.
