---
title: "AIFORIA: a COST Action for AI in fundamental physics"
subtitle: COST Action CA25172 · October 2026 – October 2030
---
AIFORIA (Artificial Intelligence for Fundamental Physics: Open Roadmapping and Inclusive Actions) builds on and is an open community activity of EuCAIF, supported by COST. It coordinates AI activities across particle, astroparticle, nuclear, gravitational-wave and cosmological physics, develops community roadmaps for the challenges these fields share, and builds capacity through training schools, hackathons and Short-Term Scientific Missions.

Participation is open. Researchers join by becoming a member of one or more of its three working groups.
